Shepherd pie

Ingredients (for two people)

  • 250g of beef mince
  • 100g of peas
  • 500g of potatoes, finely diced
  • 1x onion, finely diced
  • 1x egg
  • 1x beef stock cube
  • 1 tablespoon of mayo
  • 2 tablespoons of sweet chilli sauce
  • 1 tablespoon of worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Turn the oven on 200C gas mark. Put a pan of water to boil
  2. Fry the onion in a splash of olive oil. When soft, add the mince and cook throughly
  3. Add the peas, stock cube, sweet chilli and worcestershire sauce. Mix well with salt and pepper
  4. Once the water is boiling, add the potatoes and let them cook for 15 minutes
  5. Strain the water and add the egg, mayo, salt and pepper in the potatoes pan, then mash them.
  6. Put the cooked mince in the bottom of a large wide oven tray and arrange the mash potatoes on top until it covers the mince
  7. Oven cook for 20 minutes, then serve hot

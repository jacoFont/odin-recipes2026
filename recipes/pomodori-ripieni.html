Pomodori ripieni

Ingredients (for two people)

  • 500g lean turkey mince
  • 6x large tomatoes
  • 1x chicken stock cube
  • 1x onion, finely diced
  • 4x garlic cloves, finely diced
  • 2x mozzarella, finely sliced
  • Two tablespoons of tomato puree
  • One tablespoon of parsley
  • One tablespoon of olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Switch on the oven to 200C gas mark
  2. Trim the bases of the tomatoes so they sit flat on the work surface
  3. Slide off the top quarter of the tomatoes
  4. Using a spoon, gently scoop out the pulp and juices of the tomatoes into a bowl
  5. Fry the onion on a medium side pan after adding a spoon of oil
  6. Once the onions are mostly done, add the garlic, toasting it for a few minutes
  7. Add the turkey mince, mixing well until it is fully cooked through. Add the puree, the parsely and the stock cube
  8. Add the pulp and juices of the tomatoes inside the pan and cook for about 10 minutes
  9. Transfer the filling into the empty tomatoes and put them in the oven for 20 minutes
  10. Once done, add the mozzarella on top of the tomatoes and put back in the over for 5 minutes

Ragù pasta

Ingredients (for two people)

  • 250g of tagliatelle pasta
  • 500g mixed beef and pork mince
  • 500g tomato passata
  • 200g pancetta
  • 3x stalks of celery, finely diced
  • 2x carrots, finely diced
  • 1x onion, finely diced
  • 4x garlic cloves, finely diced
  • 1x beef stock cube
  • 250ml red wine
  • One tablespoon of tomato puree
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Start by heating a bit of oil in a large pan
  2. When hot, add onion, celery, carrots and cook on a medium heat with a pinch of salt
  3. Once become soft, add the garlic and mix until fragrant
  4. Add the pancetta and cook until tender
  5. Add the mince and cook all together, mixing frequently to ensure all the meat is throughly cooked
  6. Add the red wine on high heat and wait until the liquid disappears
  7. Add the stock cube, passata, puree, salt and pepper and mix
  8. Leave everything to boil. Once boiling, put on low heat and cover with a lid. Mix it often
  9. Boil the water for the pasta. Add the pasta and cook for 8 to 10 minutes
  10. Once the pasta is ready, strain it and mix it with the sauce
